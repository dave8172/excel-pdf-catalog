The five columns

ColumnWhat goes in itAlso accepted as
Image URL required A public link to the product photo. A photo on a white background works best — the tool trims the white away before placing it. Image, Photo, Image Src, Picture
Name required The product name, printed under the photo. Two lines at most before the type starts shrinking. Product, Title, Item
Description optional A short line under the name — spec, size, material. Desc, Details, Subtitle
Price optional A bare number prints as £; a value already starting with $, €, ₹ and so on keeps its own symbol. Unit Price, Cost, RRP
Case Size optional A bare number prints as 24 per case. Anything else — 24 x 500ml — prints exactly as typed. Case, Pack, Pack Size, Quantity
Optional really is optional. Leave out Description, Price or Case Size — or leave a single cell blank — and that line simply doesn't appear on the card. The photo takes the space instead.

Rules worth knowing

  • Header names are matched loosely — case, spaces, hyphens and underscores all fold together, so Image_URL and image url are the same thing.
  • Image URL must be a public http or https link that opens without signing in. A Google Drive or Dropbox share page is not an image link; a file path from your own computer will not work either, because your computer is not on the internet from here.
  • A product whose image fails to load still gets a card — name, description and price, with a small no image in place of the photo.
  • Completely empty rows are skipped, so gaps in the sheet are fine.
  • Up to {{ max_products }} products per file. Split a bigger list and run it twice.
  • Saving as CSV from Excel is fine — commas, semicolons and Excel's odd encodings are all handled.

2. The template PDF

The template is your page design with the product boxes left empty — your header, your colours, your footer, and boxes where products should go. The tool renders the page, finds those boxes, and draws one product inside each of them. It never invents a layout: whatever you draw is what you get back.

There is no fixed grid to match. Three boxes across or five, one row or six, boxes of different sizes on the same page — the tool measures what is actually on your page. The two templates below are somewhere to start, not a shape you have to keep.

One-page template — the simplest option

If you want a grid of products with your branding on it, one page is enough. Whatever is on that page appears on every page of the finished catalog, so keep the header to something that bears repeating.

Two-page template — a cover, then a page that repeats

Use this when the first page should look different from the rest — a cover with room for a title, then a denser page that repeats for as long as the list needs.

Download the two-page template

The rules a box has to follow

  • Draw it as a visible outline. The tool finds boxes by looking for their edges. A box implied by white space is invisible to it. Keep the outline at roughly the weight in the samples — a hairline can fall below what a page render picks up.
  • Leave it empty. A box counts only if it is drawn and still blank inside. That is deliberate: it is what stops a full-width banner or a dark footer strip from being treated as somewhere to put products.
  • Give it some size. Anything narrower than about a fourteenth of the page width, or shorter than about a twentieth of its height, is ignored — otherwise every underline and table cell on the page would be a candidate.
  • Any page size. A4, Letter, or something of your own. Boxes are found where they are, not where a template says they should be.
  • At most {{ max_template_pages }} pages. This is the page design to repeat, not the finished catalog.

3. How pages are counted

Products fill the boxes in reading order — left to right, top to bottom — and the template's pages are used in the order they appear. When the products outlast the template, the last page that has boxes on it repeats until they run out.

Your templateWhat comes back
1 page, 12 boxes, 30 products3 pages of the same design. The last has 6 boxes filled and 6 left empty.
2 pages — a 9-box cover and a 12-box inner — with 30 productsThe cover, then two of the inner page.
3 pages, 30 productsPages 1 and 2 once each, then page 3 as many times as needed.
A page with no boxes at all, in the middlePasses through untouched — a full-page photo or a divider stays where you put it, and holds no products.

5. If it complains

What it saysWhat to change
The product file needs Image URL and Name in row 1… Row 1 of the product file is not the header row, or those two headers are spelled in a way the tool does not recognise. Compare it with the downloadable template.
That product file is empty The sheet has no rows at all, or the first sheet in the workbook is the empty one. Row 1 has to be the headers.
No products were found under the header row The headers are right but every row below them is blank.
No product boxes were found in that template The boxes are not drawn as visible outlines, something is filling them, or they are too small. Start from a blank template above and redecorate around the boxes rather than moving them.
That template PDF could not be read The file is damaged or is not really a PDF. Re-export it from whatever drew it.
That template has N pages… You have uploaded a finished catalog rather than the page design to repeat. A template is at most {{ max_template_pages }} pages.
That file has more than {{ max_products }} products Split the list and run it twice.
Photos are missing from the finished PDF The image links could not be opened from the internet. Paste one into a private browser window — if it does not show a picture on its own, the tool cannot read it either.
Someone else's catalog is generating right now Exactly what it says — one catalog is built at a time. Wait a minute and press the button again.
