Columns you can add

ColumnWhat it does

Pack SizeUnit size, e.g. 500ml. With Case Size it prints as 24 x 500ml.

Adding Price or Sale Price changes the card design. Those two columns are how the tool decides you want the richer promotion card instead of the plain one. Leave them out for a photo-and-title catalog.

Rules worth knowing

  • Header names are matched loosely — case and spacing do not matter.
  • Image Src must be a public http or https link that opens without signing in. A Google Drive or Dropbox share page is not an image link; a file path from your own computer will not work either, because your computer is not on the internet from here.
  • A product whose image fails to load still gets a card — with the title and badges, minus the photo.
  • Completely empty rows are skipped, so gaps in the sheet are fine.
  • Up to {{ max_products }} products per file. Split a bigger list and run it twice.
  • Saving as CSV from Excel is fine — commas, semicolons and Excel's odd encodings are all handled.

2. The template PDF

The template is your page design with the product boxes left empty — your header, your colours, your footer, and a grid of blank boxes. The tool renders the page, finds those boxes, and draws one product inside each of them. It never invents a layout: whatever you draw is what you get back.

Three blank templates to start from. The one-page one is the simplest and the most forgiving. The other two are multi-page, and they are not interchangeable with each other — the promotion card is taller, so it fits one row fewer per page, and a promotion catalog built on the plain template comes out with an empty row on every page.

One-page template — the simplest option

If you just want a grid of products with your branding on it, one page is enough. The tool measures the rows of boxes that are actually on your page and repeats that exact layout for the whole catalog — no cover page, no back page, no rules about which row goes where. Draw one, five, or any number of rows; it uses what it finds.

The rules the grid has to follow

  • Three boxes across. The layout is a 3-column grid — that is the one thing that is not adjustable.
  • The boxes must actually be drawn, as visible outlines. The tool finds them by looking for their edges; a box implied by white space is invisible to it. Keep the outlines at roughly the weight in the sample — a hairline can fall below what a page render picks up.
  • Do not move the boxes if you start from a sample. Redecorate everything around them freely; change where they sit and the tool stops recognising the page as a template. On a one-page template you can delete whole rows you do not want — it measures what is left — but the rows that remain still have to be where they were.
  • A solid band is not a row of boxes. The tool checks that a box is drawn and still empty inside, so a full-width banner or a dark footer strip will not be mistaken for somewhere to put products.
  • Any page size works — the grid is measured in proportions, not millimetres. The sample is A4 because that is what most people will open.
  • At most {{ max_template_pages }} pages. This is the page design to repeat, not the finished catalog.

How many pages your template needs

TemplateWhat the tool does with it
1 page (one-page template)The tool measures the rows of boxes on the page and repeats that exact layout for every page of the catalog. No rules about which rows — any number, anywhere the grid allows.
2 pages (plain template)One page must be cover style and the other middle style, in either order. The cover is used first and last; the middle page repeats in between.
3 or more (promotion template)Page 1 is the cover, page 2 is the repeating middle page, and the final page is the back page. Pages between those are ignored.

3. The two settings

Card style

SettingWhat each card shows
autoPicks for you: promotion if the file has a Price or Sale Price column, por-title if it does not. Leave it here unless the result is not what you wanted.
por-titlePhoto, title, POR badge, NEW flash. The plain catalog card — use the one-page or plain template.
promotionAdds the case price, the shelf price and the pack size in a panel beside the photo — use the promotion template.
Using the plain template but your sheet has prices in it? auto will switch to the promotion card and the pages will not line up. Either set the style to por-title by hand, or take the promotion template instead. A one-page template does not have this problem — its layout is measured from the page, so both card styles fit it.

The promotion card gives roughly half its width to the price panel, so keep titles to two or three short words — a long single word cannot be wrapped and will be cut off.

Quality

normal rebuilds each page as an image — reliable, and the smaller file. high keeps your template's own text and vectors crisp and lays the products over the top, which is what you want for print. High takes longer and produces a larger PDF; try normal first.

5. If it complains

What it saysWhat to change
Missing required columns… Row 1 of the product file is not the header row, or one of Image Src / Title / POR is spelled in a way the tool does not recognise. Compare it with the downloadable template.
The PDF must contain the supported 3-column product box grid A multi-page template with no boxes the tool can find. Either they are not drawn as outlines, they have been moved, or there are not three across. Start from a blank template above.
Must contain at least one row of three empty product boxes Same thing on a one-page template. Check the boxes are drawn as visible outlines, that there are three across, and that nothing is filling them in.
Page 2 must be a middle-style 3-column grid page On a template of 3 pages or more, page 2 is the design that repeats, so it needs the full grid — not a cover or a back page.
Invalid 2-page PDF template… Both pages are the same style. A 2-page template needs one cover-style page and one middle-style page.
Every page has an empty row of boxes Plain template, promotion cards. Either switch the card style to por-title, use the promotion template, or move to a one-page template, which measures its own layout.
Photos are missing from the finished PDF The image links could not be opened from the internet. Paste one into a private browser window — if it does not show a picture on its own, the tool cannot read it either.
Someone else's catalog is generating right now Exactly what it says — one catalog is built at a time. Wait a minute and press the button again.
