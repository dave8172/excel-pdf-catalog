Catalog PDF Exporter

A product list plus your template page, back as a finished catalog PDF.

.xlsx or .csv — one product per row. Needs Image Src, Title and POR columns.

The page design, with the empty product boxes drawn on it. A single page is fine — it gets repeated for the whole catalog.

Up to {{ max_mb }} MB per upload, {{ max_products }} products per file.

Uploaded files and the finished PDF are deleted the moment the download is sent — nothing is stored.
